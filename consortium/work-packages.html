Work Packages

SWEET EDGE research is organized into thematic work packages (WPs) covering the full spectrum from energy system modelling to policy implementation and regional pilots.

WP 1

Research Focus on Cities

WP 1

WP1 analyzes how Swiss cities can design and operate multi-energy systems with the highest possible shares of locally sourced renewable energy. It examines technical aspects such as supply-demand matching, energy storage, power-to-X, electric transport, biomass, and distribution grids, alongside socio-economic factors including financing, stakeholder coordination, business models, and policy acceptance.

WP 2

Research Focus on Midlands

WP 2

WP2 explores how rural areas in the Swiss Midlands can develop multi-energy systems with high shares of locally sourced renewable energy. It examines technical aspects such as supply-demand matching, biomass, battery storage, power-to-X, electric transport and agricultural machinery, alongside distribution grids and microgrids. Particular attention is given to financing, business models, stakeholder coordination, and policy to support initiatives led by small enterprises.

WP 3

Research Focus on the Alps

WP 3

Modelling energy demand, flexibility, and grid constraints in Swiss urban areas with high building density.

WP 4

Regional Modelling — Midlands

WP 4

Analysis of the Swiss Mittelland region's potential for distributed PV, wind, and agricultural biomass integration.

WP 5

Regional Modelling — Alps

WP 5

Hydro-dominated alpine systems: pumped storage, seasonal balancing, and cross-border exchange scenarios.

WP 6

Grid Integration

WP 6

Technical assessment of distribution and transmission grid requirements under very high renewable penetration scenarios.

WP 7

Flexibility & Market Design

WP 7

Local energy markets, flexibility services, peer-to-peer trading, and demand-response mechanisms.

WP 8

Social Acceptance & Governance

WP 8

Public attitudes, community energy, and governance frameworks for decentralized renewable energy deployment.

WP 9

Policy Recommendations

WP 9

Translating research findings into actionable guidance for federal and cantonal energy policy and regulation.

WP 10

Dissemination & Knowledge Transfer

WP 10

Publications, public outreach, workshops, tools, and open-data initiatives to maximize research impact.
