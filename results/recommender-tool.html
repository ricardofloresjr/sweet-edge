Recommender Tool

An interactive decision-support tool helping municipalities and energy communities evaluate and prioritize renewable energy measures for their local context.

About the Tool

The SWEET EDGE Recommender Tool is a web-based application designed to support local decision-makers — from municipal energy managers to community planners — in identifying the most suitable renewable energy technologies and deployment strategies for their specific geographic, social, and economic context.

Users input key parameters about their community (size, climate zone, existing infrastructure, budget) and receive tailored recommendations with cost-benefit estimates, CO₂ impact, and implementation pathways.

How It Works

Step 1

Choose your municipality

Select a Swiss municipality to generate a local energy profile using available data on buildings, consumption, emissions and renewable energy potential.

Step 2

Understand the current situation

Explore maps, statistics and visualisations to identify key energy challenges, priority areas and opportunities for local action.

Step 3

Explore future pathways

Test different technologies and development scenarios, then compare their potential effects on the energy mix, emissions and the municipality's future energy system.
