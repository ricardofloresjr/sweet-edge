Policy Recommendations

Actionable guidance for federal and cantonal policymakers to accelerate Switzerland's transition to a decentralized renewable energy system.

Communicate policy effectiveness

Explain the expected impact of energy measures alongside the measures themselves to strengthen public acceptance.

Emphasize energy independence

SOLAR PV

Make subsidies more visible

SOLAR PV

Reward fuller use of rooftops

ENERGY UTILITIES & COMMUNITIES

Encourage publicly owned utilities to offer greener electricity products and pursue more ambitious energy-efficiency measures.

WIND ENERGY

Explore new locations for wind power

Assess industrial zones and off-grid areas as potentially suitable and more widely accepted locations for wind turbines.

LOCAL LEADERSHIP & MUNICIPALITIES

LOCAL PARTICIPATION

Expand citizen financing

WIND ENERGY

Streamline wind regulation

LOCAL INNOVATION

ENERGY UTILITIES & COMMUNITIES

Promote green defaults and efficiency

Use public ownership of utilities to expand green default electricity products and reward customers who make strong efficiency gains.

WIND ENERGY

Explore small and medium wind

Debate the role of small and medium-sized wind installations, particularly in industrial zones and mountainous regions.

Strengthen Energiestadt incentives

Encourage more municipalities to join Energiestadt and give renewable-energy incentives greater weight in certification requirements.

LOCAL PARTICIPATION

Expand citizen-financed energy

Use citizen financing to increase knowledge, participation and empowerment while potentially reducing opposition.

Simplify participation in local energy communities for residents and companies while addressing potential impacts on shared grid costs.

LOCAL PARTICIPATION

Use participation formats that help stakeholders reach effective compromises and record binding consent, including waivers of objections or referendums.

Use the energy independence argument

Pair the energy-transition case with energy independence to motivate a broader public to support renewable investment.

SOLAR PV

Improve awareness of PV subsidies

Ensure that the population knows which PV support schemes exist so that incentives can actually influence investment decisions.

LOCAL LEADERSHIP & MUNICIPALITIES

Pair participation with local leadership

Use participatory processes, but also foster credible local initiative and leadership that can highlight synergies and reduce perceived trade-offs.

Build on broad support for renewables through public discussion, peer networks and exchanges between early adopters and interested residents.

Reduce policy fragmentation through a harmonization and ratcheting-up process, while allowing cantons to complement federal investment grants with additional upfront support.
