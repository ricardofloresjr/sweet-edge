About the Policy White Papers

The Policy White Papers are a series of reports that provide actionable guidance for federal and cantonal policymakers to accelerate Switzerland's transition to a decentralized renewable energy system.

Each paper examines specific aspects of the energy transition, including distributional impacts, community acceptance, and market dynamics. The papers are based on rigorous analysis and stakeholder engagement to ensure their relevance and applicability.
